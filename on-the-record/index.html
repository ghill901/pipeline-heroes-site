The thesis

Pipeline is not a marketing problem or a motivation problem. It is a capacity problem with an economic bill attached, and the bill always arrives.

The plain answer

What does pipeline economics mean?

Pipeline economics asks whether a sales team has enough real capacity to create the pipeline it has been set.

It looks at the market, account list, contact data, seller time and manager support before it blames marketing or motivation. When capacity does not match the target, the cost still appears: wasted time, more tools, missed pipeline or extra headcount. That is the bill.

Quick answers

The terms behind the argument.

What is pipeline capacity?
Pipeline capacity is the amount of real selling work a team can carry out within the limits of its market, account list, contact data, seller time and management system.
What does sales development economist mean?
Gerry uses the term to describe his work on the cost, capacity and trade-offs behind outbound pipeline creation.
What is The Bill Always Arrives?
It is Gerry's forthcoming book about why pipeline capacity problems create costs even when leaders misread the cause.
Is Pipeline Heroes part of TitanX?
No. Gerry works for TitanX, but Pipeline Heroes and The Bill Always Arrives are his own work.
