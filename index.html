The Argument

Your team may be doing what the system has taught them to do.

When outbound misses, leaders often ask sellers to do more: make more calls, write better messages and take more coaching.

But the fault may sit earlier in the chain. The team may be calling the wrong accounts. Key buyers may be missing. The data may be poor. Reps may work for hours to get one useful conversation. If so, low belief is a result, not the cause.

PipelineOS helps leaders find the real break before they blame the rep.

01

Check the maths

Can the market, account list, contact data and time available produce the target?

02

Set the standard at the SKO

Use real data so leaders, managers and reps agree what will change after the event.

03

Make managers hold it

If managers go back to the old measures, reps will go back to the old work.

Why the SKO

The SKO gives you the room. Monday is the test.

At an SKO, leaders, managers and reps can focus on the same problem at the same time. That is useful. It is not change.

A keynote can help people see the problem. A working session can set a new standard. Both will fail if leaders go back to the old scorecard and old habits.

The test
Can a manager use the new standard in next week's pipeline review? If not, the SKO was a good day out.
Before the room
Agree what is wrong. Start with what the numbers show. Keep opinion separate.
In the room
Show the choices. Make clear how accounts, data, time and rep skill affect each other.
After the room
Use the new standard. Give managers a few clear questions and measures.
The boundary
Your leaders own the change. Pipeline Heroes does not become your sales or enablement team.

Frequently Asked

Questions CROs ask before they book.

How is this different from standard sales training?

Sales training starts with rep skills. PipelineOS first checks whether the system gives those reps a fair chance: the right market, accounts, buyers, data, time and manager support. Skills matter. They cannot fix every fault around them.

Can a keynote genuinely change anything?

It can help people see the same problem and agree what to do next. It cannot fix data, targets or manager habits on its own. If those are the real problems, someone inside the business must own the next step.

How do you measure whether the SKO Intervention worked?

Before the event, we agree a few clear signs of change. These will match the problem we found. After 30 days, we review what moved, what did not and why. We do not claim that one event caused every later sales result.

Does Executive Advisory include implementation?

No. We help leaders test decisions and review results. Your team runs the project, systems, data, training and daily work. If you need a team to do the work for you, this is the wrong offer.

Do we have to begin with an SKO?

No. Choose a keynote when leaders do not yet agree on the problem. Choose the SKO when the team needs a new working standard. Choose advisory when leaders are already changing the system and want support for six months.

Is the framework bespoke?

The core model stays the same. Your data, examples, exercises and measures make it yours. You get a tested framework, not a stock speech.

Is Pipeline Heroes connected to a software vendor?

Yes. Gerry leads TitanX in Europe. PipelineOS is Gerry's own framework, and buying a Pipeline Heroes service does not commit you to TitanX. If TitanX may help, Gerry will say so. You decide.
